Soccer

General Notes
  • Unless otherwise stated, all soccer bets are settled based on the result at the end of 90 minutes of regular time, plus any injury or stoppage time as awarded by the referee.
  • Extra-time and penalty shootouts are excluded unless the market explicitly states otherwise.
Market Types and Clarifications
  • 1X2 (Full-Time Result): Settlement is based on the result after 90 minutes plus injury time. Draws are valid outcomes.
  • To Qualify / To Advance: Settlement is based on the team that progresses to the next round, regardless of method (e.g., penalties or extra-time).
  • Correct Score: Based on the score at the end of regulation time.
  • Half-Time / Full-Time: Predicting the result at half-time and full-time in combination.
  • First Goalscorer / Last Goalscorer: Only players who start the match are eligible. Own goals are ignored.
  • Anytime Goalscorer: The player must be on the field at the time of the goal. Own goals do not count.
  • Next Goalscorer: Applies to the next valid goal (own goals excluded). Player must be on the field at the time.
  • Time of Goals: Goals scored in stoppage time are considered to have occurred at the end of the half (e.g., 45:00 or 90:00).
  • Booking Markets: Yellow card = 1 point, red card = 2 points. A second yellow resulting in red counts as 2 cards total. Cards shown after the match or to non-players (e.g., managers) are excluded.
  • Booking Points: Yellow = 10 pts, Red = 25 pts. A player cannot accumulate more than 35 points.
  • Corner Markets: Corners must be taken to be counted. Awarded but untaken corners do not count.
Special Considerations
  • Abandoned Matches: If not resumed within 48 hours, bets are void unless already decided.
  • Incorrect Scores: If a wrong score is published and impacts the market, Raj Poker reserves the right to void affected bets.
  • VAR (Video Assistant Referee): Events overturned by VAR may result in market resettlement or cancellation.
  • Unlisted Players: If an unlisted player scores, bets on listed players stand unless otherwise stated.
  • Extra-Time Specific Markets: Clearly marked and separate from regulation time markets.

  • If a match tie-break replaces the final set, it is considered the third set.
Settlement Specifics

  • Set Betting: Requires correct prediction of the set score.
  • Total Games / Handicap: Settled only if the full match is completed.
  • Correct Score: Must predict final set score correctly.
In-Play Specifics

General Rules
